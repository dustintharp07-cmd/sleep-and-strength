Research Arc & Observational Scope

Project Motivation & Limitations

This project began with a question rooted in athletic performance and recovery—whether habitual short sleep aligns with lower physical force—and broadened into an examination of upper-body strength across biological sex and the human lifespan (ages 16–80+).

Key study limitations: NHANES is a cross-sectional observational survey, so associations cannot establish causality or rule out reverse causation (such as underlying illness increasing sleep duration in older age). Sleep duration (SLD010H) is self-reported in whole hours rather than actigraphy-measured, and unmerged NHANES modules—such as body anthropometric measures (BMX) and physical activity (PAQ)—are outside the three-module scope of this repository.

Data Provenance

CDC/NCHS NHANES 2011–2014 Public-Use Data Sources

All analyses use public-use SAS transport (.XPT) files published by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS) across two consecutive two-year waves: 2011–2012 (Cycle G) and 2013–2014 (Cycle H). These two waves constitute the dedicated NHANES cycles in which objective bilateral handgrip dynamometry was administered in the Mobile Examination Center (MEC). Raw files are stored unmodified in data/raw/ and merged deterministically by respondent sequence number (SEQN) in scripts/prepare_data.R:

DEMO_G.XPT & DEMO_H.XPT

Demographics & Survey Design

Provides examinee age (RIDAGEYR, top-coded at 80), biological sex (RIAGENDR), race/Hispanic origin (RIDRETH3), family income-to-poverty ratio (INDFMPIR), 2-year MEC exam weights (WTMEC2YR), masked variance pseudo-strata (SDMVSTRA), and pseudo-PSUs (SDMVPSU).

SLQ_G.XPT & SLQ_H.XPT

Sleep Disorders Questionnaire

Administered during the household interview to participants aged 16 and older. Captures usual weekday/workday sleep hours (SLD010H), whether the participant ever told a doctor they had trouble sleeping (SLQ050), and physician-diagnosed sleep disorders (SLQ060).

MGX_G.XPT & MGX_H.XPT

Muscle Strength — Grip Test

Objective isometric upper-body strength measured in the MEC using a Takei Digital Grip Strength Dynamometer across three trials per hand (MGXH1T1–MGXH2T3), yielding CDC’s combined grip metric (MGDCGSZ, sum of the best reading from each hand).

Statistical Methodology

Combined NHANES Complex Survey Design & Estimation Modes

NHANES uses a stratified, multistage probability cluster design rather than simple random sampling. Following CDC/NCHS analytic guidelines for combining the 2011–2012 and 2013–2014 cycles, four-year Mobile Examination Center weights are constructed as mec_weight_4yr = WTMEC2YR / 2. Stratum identifiers (SDMVSTRA) are uniquely numbered across waves (90–103 in 2011–2012; 104–118 in 2013–2014; zero overlap), with primary sampling units (SDMVPSU) nested within strata (29 strata, 61 PSUs, 32 design degrees of freedom). To preserve full variance structure during subpopulation analysis, survey::svydesign(…, nest = TRUE) is initialized on all 19,151 MEC-examined respondents (WTMEC2YR > 0) and restricted to the 10,987-examinee cohort via subset().

Unweighted sample estimates vs. survey-weighted population estimates: Unweighted visualizations and sample statistics describe the exact 10,987 individuals examined in NHANES—a cohort in which NCHS intentionally oversamples Non-Hispanic Black, Hispanic, Non-Hispanic Asian, low-income, and older participants to ensure reliable subgroup precision. Survey-weighted population estimates reweight observations by mec_weight_4yr to represent the civilian, non-institutionalized U.S. population aged 16+ and compute Taylor-series linearized standard errors and confidence intervals accounting for PSU clustering and stratification. Every visualization and regression table on this site explicitly labels which estimation mode is displayed.

Computational Reproducibility

Reproducing the Website in a Fresh Codespace

All R package dependencies (including tidyverse, haven, survey, plotly, jsonlite, splines, broom, knitr, and scales) are declared in the root DESCRIPTION file. From a fresh GitHub Codespace or local R/Quarto environment, run the following three commands at the terminal:

1. Install all documented R dependencies (including survey >= 4.2)

Rscript -e ‘pak::local_install_deps()’

# 2. (Optional) Re-generate data/processed/nhanes_sleep_strength.rds from raw CDC XPT files
Rscript scripts/prepare_data.R

# 3. Render all six pages of the Quarto website into _site/
quarto render
